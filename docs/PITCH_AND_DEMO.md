# Submission Kit — Slides, Video, Form

## 1. Mandatory 5-Slide PPT
1. **Problem statement & understanding** — Two-sided pain (customers: unreliable, opaque pricing, safety fears; workers: inconsistent work, no digital proof of skill). One line: "We make local work **trusted, fairly priced and bill-shock-free**."
2. **Technical approach** — Next.js + React + Tailwind, Zustand store, pure logic modules (matching score, pricing/counter-offer, job state machine, OTP), EN/MR/HI i18n. Architecture diagram from TRD §3. Matching formula.
3. **Feasibility & viability** — Prototype live at <URL>; cash-on-completion + 8% platform fee model; low-cost, WhatsApp-friendly growth via Vouch links; phased roadmap (verification partners, scheduling, wage advance).
4. **Impact & benefit** — Workers: steady local jobs, own rates, portable **Skill Passport**; customers: ETA, fair price, safety (OTP, report/block); community: formalising informal work, language inclusion.
5. **Comparison with existing platforms — your unique features** — Table vs. (a) curated app-based service marketplaces, (b) listing/lead directories, (c) WhatsApp/word-of-mouth. Rows: negotiable pricing, no-surprise extra-charge approval, honest trust labels (New/Vouched/Verified), Skill Passport, regional languages, low-literacy UI. *Verify competitor claims before submitting; keep them qualitative.*
